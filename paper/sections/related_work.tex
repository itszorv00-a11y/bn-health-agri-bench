\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Cross-lingual benchmarks.} XNLI established cross-lingual
evaluation across 14 languages including relatively low-resource ones
\citep{conneau2018xnli}; however, generation-quality and safety evaluation for
Bangla-level resources remains rare \todo{add Bangla NLP citations, e.g.,
BanglaBERT and follow-ups}.

\paragraph{LLM reliability and hallucination.} Surveys document persistent
factual errors in LLM generations \citep{ji2023survey}. Automated judges built
from LLMs correlate imperfectly with human judgment \citep{zheng2023judging},
which motivates our human-verified labels.

\paragraph{Assessment methodology.} We follow standard agreement measurement
\citep{cohen1960coefficient,fleiss1971measuring,krippendorff2018content} using
the conventional interpretation bands \citep{landis1977measurement}.
