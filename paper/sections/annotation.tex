\section{Annotation Protocol}
\label{sec:annotation}

\paragraph{Annotators.} \todo{K} annotators including \todo{1 physician and 1
agricultural officer}; blind, independent labeling per the guideline
(guideline version pinned in the repository).

\paragraph{Agreement.} Pairwise Cohen's $\kappa$ and Krippendorff's~$\alpha$
computed over all (item, model, language) units. Headline annotation must
reach $\alpha \geq 0.60$ \citep{landis1977measurement}; disagreements were
adjudicated by a domain expert and reported separately.
